# Carrier logo sources

Official artwork, downloaded 2026-09-29 and **not modified** (no recoloring, cropping, tracing or
effects; the site only scales them to a uniform height). Logos are trademarks of their respective
owners. To remove a logo, set `approved: false` on the carrier in `src/config/site.ts` — the
carrier's name then shows as text.
